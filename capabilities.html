Capabilities

Independent advisory and analytical services supporting complex capital decisions across market cycles.

We provide partner-led advisory across four core practice areas, each designed to address complex capital situations where judgment, independence, and deep analytical rigor are critical to success.

Transaction Advisory

We advise clients across the full lifecycle of transactions, including acquisitions, divestitures, joint ventures, and strategic investments.

  • Mergers & acquisitions (buy-side and sell-side)
  • Strategic partnerships and joint ventures
  • Private equity and strategic investor advisory
  • Transaction structuring and negotiation support
  • Financial and commercial due diligence

Valuation Advisory

Independent valuation analysis across asset classes and transaction contexts, supporting decision-making, regulatory compliance, and stakeholder alignment.

  • Business and enterprise valuation
  • Transaction and fairness opinions
  • Valuation for fundraising, restructuring, and disputes
  • Intangible and strategic asset valuation
  • Independent review and second opinions

Corporate Finance & Capital Strategy

Advisory on capital structure, funding strategy, and long-term financial planning, supporting sustainable growth and balance sheet resilience.

  • Capital structure optimisation
  • Equity and debt fundraising support
  • Financial modelling and scenario analysis
  • Strategic planning and capital allocation
  • IPO and public market readiness

Independent Research & Analysis

Objective, conflict-free analysis on companies, sectors, and capital themes, designed for institutional and professional audiences.

  • Company and sector research
  • Investment and risk analysis
  • Market structure and competitive assessment
  • Long-cycle thematic research
  • Regulatory and policy impact analysis
